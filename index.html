What I work on

Four areas where mapping, money and open technology meet — each aimed at making environmental work easier to fund and easier to trust.

Regenerative finance

Funding models that pay for ecosystem restoration — carbon and environmental credits recorded on blockchains, community currencies, and finance tied to measurable impact.

Environmental intelligence

Using satellites, sensors and decentralized networks to monitor, report and verify what is actually happening on the land — and turning that into data people can act on.

Protocols & decentralized finance

The plumbing behind it: stablecoins, treasury design, decentralized exchanges and automation that moves money across many blockchains without middlemen.

Private & open AI

AI you can run and own yourself — local models, agents, and decentralized computing and storage instead of rented platforms.
